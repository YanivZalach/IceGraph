## Contributions

Contributions are accepted only after the contributor signs the
[IceGraph Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). The CLA leaves ownership with
the contributor while giving the project rights to relicense contributions,
including under source-available and proprietary terms. Contributions also
remain available under the project license in effect when they were submitted.

## Commercial and enterprise licensing

Separate commercial licenses and private enterprise features may be offered in
the future. For licensing questions, contact the project through
https://github.com/YanivZalach/IceGraph/issues.
